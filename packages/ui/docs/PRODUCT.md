# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Field sales representatives, warehouse and inventory teams, sales managers, finance and IT administrators, and executives—often working quickly, on mobile devices, or with intermittent connectivity.

## Product Purpose

Sunpride Foods is a fully integrated Philippine meat processor. Its digital products should make sales, inventory, distribution, and customer service feel dependable, efficient, and easy to use.

## Brand Personality

Clear, confident, warm, and practical. Use plain language, concise action labels, and reassuring confirmations. Professional without sounding bureaucratic or overly technical.

## Anti-References

- Do not use Sunpride yellow for large backgrounds or body text.
- Avoid excessive red; reserve it for primary actions and key emphasis.
- No decorative gradients, glassmorphism, or heavy shadows in operational screens.
- Do not hide critical data or actions behind hover-only interactions.

## Strategic Design Principles

- Keep operational screens predominantly neutral so inventory, order, and sales data remain easy to scan.
- Use sentence case and short, action-oriented labels.
- Use semantic colors consistently: green for success, yellow for warning, dark red for danger.
- Design mobile flows for one-handed use and make offline, syncing, synced, and failed states unmistakable.
- Use tables for comparable operational data, tabular numerals for quantities and currency, and persistent filters for active views.
- Meet WCAG AA contrast and never rely on color alone to communicate status.

## Accessibility Needs

Maintain clear information hierarchy, keyboard-accessible interactions, readable copy, and sufficient contrast across product-critical flows.
