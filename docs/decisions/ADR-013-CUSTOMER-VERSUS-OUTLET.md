# ADR-013: Customer accounting master versus outlet execution profile

Status: accepted (2026-09-25, JC lead) — provisional items marked inline

## Context

`ARCH-010` needs a stable distinction between an accounting customer and a physical selling site. The platform blueprint §5 separates the SAP customer master from the Turbo outlet profile and §44 assigns accounting master to SAP, GPS, routes and visits to Turbo. Its older MCP example (§4) says `customerId` on a plan outlet; this ADR resolves the execution target to an **outlet ID**, not a billing account ID. The Sales Head memo §3 requires the MCP to reflect the stores visited and grounds per diem in approved planned calls; Annex A requires preparation and approval. Annexes B–C carry account/customer details in reports and call sheets (memo §7), but do not establish ERP authority for outlet execution attributes. Existing `customers`, `salesAssignments`, `visits` and `orders` still use customer codes and lack a distinct outlet ID (`packages/backend/convex/schema.ts:95-106,1036-1068`). This is a design decision, not a statement that the migration is complete.

## Decision

| Subject                   | Authority and identity                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Examples                                                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer/accounting party | SAP is authoritative. Store the stable SAP customer ID/code, legal/billing name and address, tax classification, payment/credit terms and limit, and accounting status as a synchronized projection; preserve source version and sync time. No field edit overwrites these attributes (blueprint §§5, 44; ADR-003 identifier ownership).         | Invoiced party, credit account.                            |
| Outlet/execution site     | Turbo is authoritative. Assign a Turbo outlet ID independent of customer code; own name used in field, GPS/geofence and verification, address/location directions, photos, contacts, channel/subchannel/store classification, territory/route, visit window/frequency, merchandising attributes and active/prospect state (blueprint §§3–5, 44). | One chain branch, market stall or prospect shop.           |
| Link                      | Zero or one current SAP customer per outlet; one SAP customer may link to many outlets. Keep dated link history and provenance; never identify an outlet by customer code alone. **Provisional — pending client confirmation** for shared premises, joint billing or multiple legal payers.                                                      | One account, multiple branches; prospect with no customer. |

- MCP slots, territory assignment, visits, location evidence and outlet coverage refer to `outletId`; orders and SAP submissions carry the resolved SAP customer identifier as a separate field. Existing orders' customer-code-only shape must not silently choose a branch. The customer link at order creation is snapshotted for audit; later remapping cannot silently change an approved transaction (blueprint §§4–5, 44; ADR-002 server-owned lifecycle).
- A **prospect outlet** may be created, assigned to a route, planned and visited without an SAP customer. It has a Turbo ID and explicit `prospect` status; its local name/address are lead data, not a fabricated SAP accounting customer. Prospect-to-customer conversion links an approved SAP customer record to the existing outlet without rewriting past visits or MCP slots. **Provisional — pending client confirmation** for prospect creation/approval and linkage workflow. Until linked and approved, a prospect cannot produce an ERP-postable order, credit decision, collection or financially recognized van sale; capture lead interest as non-financial activity only (ADR-003; ADR-008). The client memo §2's channel execution criteria still require evidence before a prospect visit can count as productive; no automatic productivity from a lead note.
- SAP inbound upsert may change customer fields and deactivate a customer, but must not overwrite outlet-owned fields or delete visits. On inactive/missing/unmatched customer IDs, quarantine affected new financial transactions and surface an exception; preserve the historic link and non-financial visits. Offline clients use versioned cached links and verify current customer/credit eligibility server-side before approval or SAP posting, never trust a client-supplied customer ID as authorization (ADR-002; ADR-005).
- Outlet visibility follows the server-enforced organizational subtree (ADR-005); a customer shared across territories does not grant cross-territory access to its outlets. A national customer view may aggregate only through authorized scope (ADR-009). **Provisional — pending client confirmation** on cross-territory ownership of shared accounts.

## Consequences

- `CVX-007` adds outlet identity, optional customer link, operational attributes, uniqueness and dated link/audit records. `SAP-004` maps the SAP-authoritative accounting projection and sync conflict handling; the connector audit confirms customer pull/upsert is not implemented (`apps/sap-connector/docs/SFA_READINESS.md`, Delivery gaps SAP-004). Design unique SAP external IDs independently of outlet IDs.
- `CVX-008`, `CVX-011`, `CVX-012`, mobile bootstrap and analytics migrate from raw customer codes to outlet IDs and preserve historic plan/visit attribution; read both identifiers during migration, backfill only unambiguous mappings, and flag the rest for review. Existing schema fields are not silently reinterpreted. Reports can still group by customer via the dated link (`schema.ts:1036-1068`; blueprint §§4–5, 21).
- Orders/van POS require an explicit validated outlet-to-customer resolution before posting; prospect visits remain available in both native field apps while financial checkout is blocked for unlinked prospects. The separate Android POS boundary follows ADR-010. ERP financial and pricing authority remain ADR-003 and ADR-008, not this ADR.

## Open questions

- **New:** Which team approves prospect creation, deduplication and SAP-customer linkage, and may outlets have multiple legal payers or cross-territory ownership?
- **New:** What is the SAP customer/ship-to key structure and how are branch identities reconciled with Turbo outlets? This requires SAP-team sample data and is not settled by the memo.
- **Client questions 2–3, 5:** confirm prospect visit productivity, call identity and per diem exceptions; no entitlement is inferred from this ADR.
