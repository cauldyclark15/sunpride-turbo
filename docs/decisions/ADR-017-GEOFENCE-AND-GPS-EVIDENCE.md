# ADR-017: Geofence validation and GPS evidence for visits

Status: accepted (2026-09-25, JC lead) — provisional items marked inline

## Context

A check-in needs location evidence rather than an unverified tap. The blueprint proposes outlet-specific geofences, accuracy/provider/mock flags and event-only tracking (`docs/architecture/SFA_PLATFORM_BLUEPRINT.md` §§5, 10–11, 53). The client memo requires documented MCP calls for per diem (Sales Operations Standards memo §3) but does **not** prescribe a radius, GPS threshold, retention schedule or location-surveillance policy. ADR-016 owns visit state and actual/productive-call certification; ADR-015 owns MCP lifecycle.

## Decision

**Provisional — pending client confirmation:** engineering default radius **75 m**, overridable per outlet (malls, warehouses) by authorized manager with reviewed, effective-dated record. Keep pin source, verifier, time, radius/version and change reason; handset values are untrusted. Use pin/radius effective at event time, never retroactively invalidate visits. Unlike blueprint §10's 75–200 m warning band, **every** out-of-radius sample needs review before call certification.

**Provisional — pending client confirmation:** require a foreground fix no older than **60 seconds** with horizontal accuracy **≤50 m**. Compare WGS84 great-circle distance between sample and outlet pin to the effective radius; the accuracy number is a quality gate, **not** extra distance allowance. Record distance, radius and threshold in meters. Failed acquisition, stale fix, low accuracy, outside radius, impossible coordinates or suspected mock location cannot auto-validate; the rep may continue recording work with a visible `location_pending_review` badge, but no verified actual call, productive credit or per-diem evidence until disposition. Apply the same policy at check-out; lack of check-out proof goes to review rather than auto-completion. Use the best available platform provider, but do not imply network/fused estimates are GPS-satellite fixes (blueprint §§10–11).

Each immutable check-in/out event retains visit/outlet/person/device IDs; provider (`gps`, `network`, `fused`, `unknown`); coordinates, accuracy, device/server times, online status, pin/radius and policy versions, calculated distance, result and mock/tamper signals. Server recomputes distance, validates scope/order and ignores device `withinGeofence`. Offline events are encrypted, idempotent and **provisional** until sync (ADR-002, ADR-010; blueprint §§10–11). Clock drift/implausible travel flags review, not proof of fraud.

Android records OS mock-location flags where available (`Location.isMock`, including compatibility paths), plus developer/test-build indicators; a positive flag blocks auto-validation. iOS Core Location does not offer a universal spoof-proof assertion: use `CLLocation.sourceInformation?.isSimulatedBySoftware` when available, reject simulator/test-provider evidence in production, and flag impossible jumps or device-integrity warnings for review. An absent/false flag on either OS is **not** proof of genuine position. Do not assert jailbreak/root detection as reliable; a human reviews contested evidence (blueprint §11).

Exception flow: rep selects a governed code from ADR-016 (`gps_unavailable`, `poor_accuracy`, `out_of_radius`, `outlet_pin_wrong`, `access_restricted`, `mock_location_suspected`), supplies a note and optional outlet photo/context; submit offline if necessary. A scoped manager with a new `visit.locationException.approve` capability reviews original evidence, alternate corroboration, route/MCP context and rep explanation. The manager approves or rejects with recorded actor, time and rationale; no self-approval, no mutation of the original fix. A mock-positive event **always** requires independent corroboration and manager review, never a one-tap override. Approval validates this one event/visit, not a global radius or location change. Rejection leaves the visit recorded but unverified; an ADR-016 correction workflow handles subsequent evidence. An exception alone cannot satisfy the memo's productive-call checklist (§2), and a GPS override does not make an off-plan call per-diem eligible (memo §3; Client question 5).

Privacy by design: only explicit foreground check-in/check-out events are captured during an authorized work period; **no passive location sampling, route breadcrumb, continuous background tracking, or tracking outside work hours**. No location permission request until a rep invokes the visit action; the mobile app should use while-in-use permission, not always/background permission. If an employee invokes a visit outside their authorized shift, record non-location visit intent for supervisor review; do not collect coordinates until an authorized work window is established. **Provisional — pending Client question 8:** Saturday and other work-window policy must follow confirmed schedules, not assume every role works the same week. A supervisor sees visit events and exceptions, not a live employee map (blueprint §§11, 18, 53).

**Provisional — pending client privacy/legal confirmation:** encrypt precise event coordinates at rest and in transit; restrict row access to the rep, scoped supervisor and audited authorized operations; delete raw coordinates and optional geotagged exception media **90 days after visit closure**, or **365 days after exception resolution** for disputed events, unless a documented legal hold applies. Remove raw coordinates from device after confirmed sync, retaining only the minimal queued command until acknowledgment; never put them in analytics/logs/SAP exports. Keep non-coordinate outcome, reason, approver and aggregate counts under the organization's ordinary record policy. Provide subject-access/deletion procedures subject to legal hold. These retention intervals are engineering defaults, not statutory claims.

## Consequences

- Add outlet geolocation/radius/version, location events, exception decisions and retention/deletion jobs; `visits` currently has only `planned/completed/cancelled` and no evidence (`packages/backend/convex/schema.ts`). Index for scoped review, never open coordinate queries nationally by default.
- Add scoped exception capability to `packages/backend/convex/lib/capabilities.ts` (ADR-009); preserve analyst read-only permissions and audit every raw-coordinate access. Both native field apps implement equivalent capture, disclosure and offline UI (ADR-010).
- Check-in/out and per-diem interfaces must distinguish provisional, verified and exception-approved events; reporting and finance must not count an unreviewed location as verified (memo §3).

## Open questions

Confirm client radius/accuracy and outlet override owner; privacy/legal retention and work-window policy. Client question 8 covers Saturday schedules; the remaining confirmation requests are new and do not imply that the memo answered them.
