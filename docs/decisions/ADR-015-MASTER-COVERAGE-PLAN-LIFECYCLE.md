# ADR-015: Monthly Master Coverage Plan lifecycle (ARCH-012)

Status: accepted (2026-09-25, JC lead) — provisional items marked inline

## Context

The memo requires a plan of the stores KAS/Booking personnel will visit on each day, a named truck for Distributor Specialist Work-With, and per diem validation strictly against the approved, planned monthly MCP plus reports/documentation (`docs/requirements/SALES_OPS_STANDARDS_MEMO_2026-01-20.md` §3). Annex A is a month/week/day grid with Prepared by and Approved by signatures (§7, Annex A). The blueprint describes a versioned MCP and planned visits (`docs/architecture/SFA_PLATFORM_BLUEPRINT.md` §§3–4, 20, 34). ADR-014 supplies effective-dated territory/route/outlet assignments; ADR-009 supplies roles and capabilities.

## Decision

- **One plan per person per calendar month and version.** Key it by organization, assigned employee and local month (recommended system default: `Asia/Manila`), with monotonically increasing version, effective start/end within that month, owning org unit, planner and approver identities/timestamps. Keep all versions; at most one active version covers any employee/date. A month can include several nonoverlapping active versions after approved changes. Use "Master Coverage Plan (MCP)" in the UI. **Provisional — pending Client question 4** on the client's "Master"/"Monthly" naming (memo §3 note, Annex A).
- **Draft → submitted → approved → active → superseded.** A preparer with scoped `mcp.plan` builds the month grid (outlet, planned date, employee, route/order, objective/frequency and non-visit activity); submit records a frozen proposal for review. A scoped supervisor with `mcp.approve` approves as a distinct person; `manager` is the normal supervisor role, `super_admin` only a controlled substitute. A supervisor can return a submitted plan to draft with reasons; a changed proposal requires resubmission. Approval records the immutable signed content and version. The approved plan becomes active at its effective start, never before approval; activation is an idempotent server action/scheduled transition with actor/time audit, not a new business approval. An approved future version remains approved until its effective start. Superseding an active version ends it immediately before the successor's effective start. No overlapping active dates or silent activation of an unapproved version (memo §3, §7 Annex A; blueprint §4; ADR-009).
- **Plan content and validation.** The monthly grid includes selling visits and admin/coaching/Work-With activities. KAS/Booking should show the five stores for each selling day and succeeding planned days (memo §3); five is the client standard, while which roles/days it governs remains **Provisional — pending Client questions 1, 7 and 8** (memo §§2, 5). Distributor Specialist Work-With identifies the truck (memo §3); do not treat a truck identifier as a sales route or require a future POS trip to exist. Validate assigned outlets/routes and employee scope on each planned date using ADR-014's temporal assignments, flag conflicts and missing days, and show position-specific weekly routines as editable templates, not hard-coded approval rules (memo §5; blueprint §§3–4). The exact call/attendance counting rule is **Provisional — pending Client question 2**.
- **Approved snapshot → planned visits.** On activation, deterministically create or reconcile one planned-visit record per plan version, employee, date and outlet/slot with a stable idempotency key, approved plan version, route/sequence and intended activities. Re-running activation cannot duplicate visits. An actual call links the planned visit if it matches; an off-plan call remains an actual call marked unplanned, never inserted into an approved MCP by the device (blueprint §§4, 20–22, 34; memo §3). Separate non-visit activities from outlet visits.
- **After approval, no in-place edits** to dates, outlet/truck, employee, order, objectives, assignments or signatures. A mid-month change creates a new draft version with reason/diff and future effective date; it follows submission and independent approval, then atomically supersedes the remaining future schedule. Past and completed visits, prior approved versions and submitted evidence stay intact. Future unexecuted visits displaced by the new version are explicitly cancelled/replaced with provenance, not deleted; an offline completion against a displaced visit is retained for review rather than overwritten (ADR-002; blueprint §34). Do not backdate a revision into an elapsed day.
- **Per diem is a validation gate, not automatic payment.** Only calls/activities on the applicable approved planned version, supported by required reports/documentation, qualify for validation; show unmet or off-plan items as exceptions, do not pay/approve them automatically (memo §3). Rate, claimant eligibility, financial approval chain and any off-plan exception entitlement are **Provisional — pending Client question 5**. `perdiem.approve` is distinct from `mcp.approve`; MCP approval does not approve a claim (ADR-009; `packages/backend/convex/lib/capabilities.ts`).

## Consequences

- Add versioned plans, immutable approval snapshots, activity/visit slots, planned-visit generation keys and replacement lineage, approval/activation audit, and scoped `mcp.plan`/`mcp.approve` endpoints (`CVX-008`–`CVX-011`, `WEB-009`–`WEB-013`, `WEB-018`, `WEB-025`, `SOP-003`). Current `visits` and `salesAssignments` have no MCP version or operational outlet linkage; migrate without treating legacy rows as approved (`packages/backend/convex/schema.ts`).
- Sync the active plan and revisions to both native field apps as server-authoritative data; mobile offline execution cannot change approval state (ADR-002, ADR-010; blueprint §34). Preserve approved-plan snapshots for per diem audit (`SOP-004`).

## Open questions

- Client questions 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 12 determine standards/naming, per diem exceptions, operating days and whether this memo remains current. Until answered, the defaults above are engineering proposals, not claims of Sunpride policy beyond memo §3.
- How far ahead must a monthly MCP be submitted/approved, and who substitutes for an absent supervisor? **Provisional — pending client confirmation.** No deadline or delegated authority is inferred from Annex A's signatures.
