# ADR-019: Offline business guarantees for field and van sales

Status: accepted (2026-09-25, JC lead) — provisional items marked inline

## Context

The native iOS and Android field apps and separate Android van POS (ADR-010) cannot assume a live connection. The memo requires channel-specific productive-call evidence, an approved MCP for per diem, and field reporting (Sales Operations Standards memo §§2–3, 6–7). The blueprint proposes a local database, outbox, scoped bootstrap, and explicit sync (§§29–38, 75–77). This decision defines what a user can actually complete without making the device the authority.

## Decision

An authenticated, registered device must first complete an online bootstrap of its assigned scope, current approved plan, applicable catalog/prices, and (for POS) an opened route with verified load. A fresh install, login for a new user, device registration, assignment change, or first route opening requires connectivity. No offline credential reauthentication is claimed: an existing locally unlocked session may continue only under ADR-020's finite offline lease.

Acknowledgment and authoritative inventory posting occur in Convex. | Both: sync status and history                                                                                                                                                           | Show local saved count and most recent server acknowledgment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Never label a queued record “Synced” or show cached SAP/stock as live.                                                                                                                       |

Offline sale authorization fails closed if the device lacks a valid route, sellable lot/quantity, applicable effective price/UOM, or remaining offline lease. Do not silently use an expired promotion or estimate a discount (ADR-003, ADR-007, ADR-008). Cached truck projection decrements atomically with the sale and never goes negative; only Convex `postMovement` commits authoritative stock. Locally paid cash remains a custody obligation, not proof of server settlement.

Each write commits data and outbox in one local transaction. Preserve the order UUID as `clientRequestId`; POS additionally sends route, stable device ID and monotonic sequence. Convex deduplicates, verifies ordering, price/scope/stock and returns per-record accepted/rejected/conflict outcomes. Server-owned approval, SAP number, inventory ledger and MCP version always win. Do not rewrite submitted intent; route rejections for review or compensating workflows. This extends ADR-002's policy to native SQLite/Room without claiming IndexedDB/browser timers are reusable. Preserve legacy queued records through upgrades (ADR-002, ADR-003; blueprint §§32–35).

Retry on connectivity restoration and periodic background/foreground wake with single-flight ordered batches, jittered bounded exponential backoff (initial 5 seconds, cap 15 minutes, engineering defaults); resume without duplicate posting. After five failed attempts expose a **needs attention** state consistent with ADR-002; continue safe retries after connectivity returns, never delete or auto-merge a rejected record. Queue survives app/process reboot. Users see `Saved on device • N pending`, `Syncing X/N`, `Synced at <server time>`, or `Needs review`; show last successful refresh and last price/plan/stock refresh separately. Manual Retry and exportable support diagnostics exclude secrets and customer financial data.

**Provisional — pending client confirmation:** local cache validity is 24 hours from last successful bootstrap for field execution and 24 hours for an opened POS route; at expiry allow reading locally saved evidence and retrying sync, but block new sale/collection/order creation until refreshed. Warn at 4 hours remaining. Device offline lease itself is defined in ADR-020. The 24-hour limit is a risk-control default, not a Sunpride policy. Where the two limits differ, the stricter limit wins.

## Consequences

- Build shared versioned sync contracts, scoped bootstrap, persistent outbox/cursor and a rejection-review surface (`CVX-018`–`CVX-022`, iOS/Android/VAN workstreams). Existing `inventory/pos.ts` route sequence/idempotency and `domains/orders.ts` client pricing are not a complete mobile sync gateway; enforce authoritative pricing before offline pilot (ADR-008).
- Test airplane-mode workflows, app kill/restart, duplicate pushes, ordered POS replay, lease/cache expiry, revocation on reconnect, price change and stock conflict. Paper and cash reconciliation must remain possible when a queued sale is rejected.

## Open questions

- Sunpride must approve offline cache/lease duration, price and promotion expiry policy, whether a locally printed pending receipt is acceptable and its required legal wording, and who resolves rejected cash sales. These are new questions beyond Client questions 1–12.
- Productive-call checklist specifics and call counting remain **Provisional — pending Client questions 2 and 3**; no offline scoring rule may hard-code an unresolved definition.
