# SFA glossary

Canonical vocabulary for coverage, visits, and van sales (`ARCH-008`). The client's Sales Operations Standards memo supplies channel-specific productive-call criteria, role targets, MCP/per diem rules, and Work-With requirements (`docs/requirements/SALES_OPS_STANDARDS_MEMO_2026-01-20.md` §§2–4). The metric grain and unresolved choices below are **Provisional** until business sign-off; see `KPI_DEFINITIONS.md`. The platform model is a design reference, not a client mandate (`SFA_PLATFORM_BLUEPRINT.md` §§3–5, 21–22). ADR-009 separates app role, position, and org scope; ADR-010 selects native iOS/Android field apps and a separate Android van POS.

Use one agreed term consistently after sign-off; keep the memo's unexpanded acronyms pending Client question 9 rather than guessing.

## Memo positions and channels

These are **positions/activities, not app roles** (ADR-009; memo §§2, 4–5). `KA` means Key Accounts (memo §5.2), `GT` General Trade (memo §§2, 5.2), and `PMOT` Public Market / Open Trade (memo §2). Do not merge ADP as a partner organization with ADP personnel.

## Memo forms and acronyms

The memo calls the monthly plan both **Master Coverage Plan** (Annex A) and **Monthly Coverage Plan** (§3). Use **MCP** and Master Coverage Plan provisionally, **pending Client question 4**. Its planned-activity evidence governs per diem (memo §3); this glossary does not set rates.
