# Management exception dashboard (`ANA-007`)

Where: web **Reports** (`/analytics`), below the daily execution dashboard, for supervision readers (`people.read` + `visit.read` + `report.read`: super admin, admin, manager, analyst, viewer). Backend: `packages/backend/convex/analytics/exceptions.ts` (`field`, `geofence`, `operations`, `outOfStock`); pure rules and every threshold in `analytics/exception_model.ts`, mirrored by `apps/web/src/lib/management-exceptions.ts` (a test keeps them equal).

Managers manage exceptions instead of inspecting every transaction (blueprint §58). Filters: a period of Manila dates (default month to date, at most 31 days), unit (inside the caller's own scope, never wider), channel and "My team only". Channel and My team narrow the people lists; unit narrows everything. Each list is its own query inside its own error boundary, so one failing list never blanks the others.

## Lists

| List                      | What is shown                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Missed high-value outlets | Planned stops (signed MCP, status `planned`) of days that have closed at 10 PM with no completed visit, where the outlet is high value: classification A/AA/AAA/A+/KA/Key account/Platinum/Gold, or channel KA/KAG/Key account/Modern trade. Today's stops are pending, never missed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Behind plan               | Field people materially behind: under 80% of planned stops done on closed days (judged only with at least 5 such stops), or under 80% of the sales target on closed days. Target = the daily `sales_value` target, else the monthly target spread over the month's selling days (Daily Sales Report rule); sales by the day written.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Repeated location issues  | Check-in/out fixes outside the radius, unavailable or unreliable in the period, for people and outlets with 3 or more. "To review" = awaiting a supervisor decision. Fake-location signals are counted. The client set no check-in distance (call answer 13): these trace a day, they never block it.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| SAP failures              | Integration events `failed` or `dead_letter`, and `pending`/`processing` events older than 2 hours whose next retry is due; connectors not `online` or silent for 15 minutes. National view only (needs `integration.read`): events carry no unit, so a regional view would be partial and is shown as unavailable.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Unclosed trips            | Van route sessions still loading/open/closing/reconciling after the 10 PM close of the day they opened (call answer 14), plus every `review_required` trip (its route-close count disagreed). Scoped by the truck location's unit; needs `inventory.read`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Stock variances           | Stock counts at locations in scope with any non-zero line variance: counts awaiting approval (`submitted`/`reviewed`) whatever their date, and approved/posted counts of the period. Open SAP reconciliation differences by classification; differences without a mapped location are national only. Blind counts keep approver separation (as `inventory/counts.ts` detail): a reader who started or counted a blind count never gets its variance or signs; the list only says how many were withheld. |
| Cash variances            | Not shown: the field apps cannot record cash collected yet (`collection.record` is refused) and there is no record of cash handed in, so there is nothing to compare. Blocked on Sunpride's cash reconciliation documents (SP-0032 / ARCH-007). The list says so instead of showing an invented zero.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Out-of-stock hotspots     | Merchandising audit findings `out_of_stock` (not low stock or not carried) in the period: outlets out of stock 3 or more times, products out of stock in 3 or more outlets, and findings per unit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

## Limits and open points

- Computed live from the period's records; there are no rollups yet (`CVX-031`). Field people are read in pages of 6 (each its own query, a month of one person's stops, visits and orders); a scope is capped at 80 people like Supervision. Location fixes (2,000), shelf findings (4,000), inventory locations (150) and SAP events (200 per status) have read caps; a notice asks to pick a unit when a cap is hit.
- Every threshold is provisional until Sunpride answers: what "high value" means (their outlet classes), what "materially behind" means (80% here), and what "repeated" means (3 here). They live in `exception_model.ts` only.
- Cash variance needs field cash collection capture plus a cash remittance record (van POS settlement); neither is built, and their design waits on Sunpride's cash reconciliation documents (SP-0032).
- Productive-call shortfalls stay on the daily execution and supervisor productivity dashboards; this view looks at plan completion and sales.
