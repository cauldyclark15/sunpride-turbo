# SFA non-functional requirements (ARCH-023)

Status: engineering acceptance targets, **provisional pending client volumes, security and SLA approval**. Measure these in pilot/load tests; they are not observed production performance.

Scope: management web, native iOS/Android field apps, Android van POS, Convex operational backend and Bun SAP connector (ADR-010). Convex owns operational stock and SAP owns accounting outcomes (ADR-003). Device offline promises are in ADR-019, device trust and lease in ADR-020. The blueprint's mobile local-store/outbox/explicit sync (§§29–38), scoped bootstrap (§§37–38), POS flow (§§23–28, 73–75) and audit events (§§49–50) inform these proposed targets.

## Sizing assumptions and capacity

The client's Sales Operations Standards memo §2 lists 5 calls/day at 90% productive for KAS/booking and 30 calls/day at 85% for route/PMOT groups; the row overlap and definition of a call are **Provisional — pending Client questions 1 and 2**. These numbers are targets, not measured traffic. For planning only, assume **200 five-call and 600 thirty-call sellers nationwide**, 800 active mobile users; neither headcount nor mix has been supplied by Sunpride. Peak concurrency, number of trucks and non-sales admin users are unknown.

`200 × 5 + 600 × 30 = 19,000 calls/day`. Assuming 26 active days in a planning month gives `19,000 × 26 = 494,000 calls/month`; Saturday inclusion remains **Provisional — pending Client question 8**. If one call emits ten separately synchronized visit/activity/order/evidence metadata operations, plan for **190,000 operations/day**. Over eight active hours that is `190,000 ÷ (8 × 3,600) ≈ 6.6 operations/s`; test **10× burst, at least 66 accepted operations/s**, plus simultaneous bootstrap/delta reads. Ten operations per call and the 10× burst are ours, not memo rules; large photos are uploads with separate storage/bandwidth budgets. Do not conflate a productive-call percentage with an extra event count.

## Operations and open inputs

Instrument device queue age/count, bootstrap age, last acknowledgment, push acceptance/rejection, POS route sequence gaps, Convex latency/error/quota, printer failures, SAP effect lag/dead letters and audit archive integrity. Alert when p95 sync delay or connector lag breaches target for 15 minutes, any route sequence gap blocks posting, or a revoked device attempts access. Give regional operators a reconciliation queue, not a silent retry loop.

**New inputs beyond Client questions 1–12:** actual headcount by role/region, truck/device quantities, site connectivity, peak media size/volume, incident/security owner, receipt traffic, retention schedule and legal hold rules, approved uptime/SAP maintenance windows, and target pilot device/network classes. Recalculate capacity and revise targets after measured pilot data.
