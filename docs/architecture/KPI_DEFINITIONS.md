# SFA KPI definitions (`ANA-001`)

**Business sign-off draft.** The memo (`docs/requirements/SALES_OPS_STANDARDS_MEMO_2026-01-20.md` §2) sets daily call and productive-call targets and channel execution standards, but does not prescribe every denominator or data-quality rule. Choices marked **Provisional** are recommended engineering defaults, not claims about Sunpride policy. The blueprint (`SFA_PLATFORM_BLUEPRINT.md` §§21–22) distinguishes planned, actual, effective and productive calls. Use the glossary (`SFA-GLOSSARY.md`); do not treat “effective” as a synonym for “productive.”

## Counting contract

- **Grain:** a planned call is one approved MCP slot `(plan version, assignee, outlet, local service date, slot ID)`; a raw actual visit has one immutable visit ID. For KPI credit, collapse eligible completed/verified visits to one call per `(rep, outlet, local service date)`, retaining all underlying visit IDs for audit. Draft/cancelled/in-progress records do not count. Offline command idempotency prevents replay. **Provisional — pending Client question 2.**
- **Local date:** attribute actuals by verified service/check-in time in `Asia/Manila`, not server receipt time. Keep device and server timestamps, sync status, plan version and immutable evidence. Reject impossible or unverified times for review rather than silently dating by sync time. **Provisional — pending client confirmation.**
- **Matching:** link at most one completed visit to a planned slot of the same rep, outlet and service date; unmatched visits are off-plan. A plan revision only affects slots prospectively once approved; retain the plan version that applied on the service date. Counts never arise from mere check-in, order rows or photos alone. **Provisional — pending Client questions 2 and 5.** MCP approval and per diem evidence follow memo §3 and Annex A.
- **Aggregation:** classify each credited call after collapsing repeats, then sum by local day and rep; territory uses the territory assigned to the visited outlet at service time, with immutable attribution, and higher scopes sum each credited call once. For multi-territory managers aggregate distinct credited call keys, never averages of percentages. Prospects are outlets and may count if in the approved plan; their lack of SAP customer ID does not make a visit invalid (ADR-013). **Provisional — pending client confirmation.**

### Productive-call checklist

The memo §2 says the channel-specific standards **must** be met. Recommended default: require evidence for every applicable item in the row, not simply a nonzero sale. Record `satisfied`, `not satisfied`, or a supervisor-approved `not applicable` with reason per item; no silent auto-pass. Until ICO's action set and channel-specific evidence are signed off, report a provisional classification and disclose missing evidence; do not use it for disciplinary or financial decisions (**Provisional — pending Client questions 2–3 and 12**).

| Channel / call type             | Required evidence from memo §2                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Chain store with PO releasing   | PO retrieval, ICO intervention, merchandising fixes, price execution, collection and promo execution.              |
| Store without PO retrieval      | Offtake/inventory and ICO fixes, merchandising fixes, price execution and promo validation.                        |
| PMOT (public market/open trade) | Sales generation (volume/distribution), display/price tagging, price compliance, collection and program execution. |

A zero-value order is **not** automatic productivity; only applicable evidenced checklist items decide. A later return does not erase the historical visit or retroactively reverse its on-site execution; show returned/net sales separately, with an audit-linked correction only for erroneous evidence. Both are **Provisional — pending Client question 2**. Pricing and financial value are not field-app authority (ADR-008).

## Edge and correction rules

- **Rescheduled:** an approved pre-service replan replaces the old due slot without double counting. A change after service preserves the old plan as history and records the miss/exception; no retroactive rewrite of per diem basis. **Provisional — pending Client question 5** (memo §3).
- **Split/repeated visits:** separate completed physical visits get separate IDs and appear in raw audit, but at most one counts as the rep/outlet/day call and one matches a planned slot; combine verified same-day evidence across the group's visits for checklist/objective classification. Additional contacts are flagged repeated, not extra target credit. Coverage remains one distinct outlet. **Provisional — pending Client question 2.**
- **Off-plan:** eligible completed visits contribute to actual, productive/effective counts and strike rate but not planned slots, plan completion or planned-outlet coverage. Their claim status is held for review; not automatically payable (memo §3; **Provisional — pending Client question 5**).
- **Late offline sync:** pending local visits are absent from server-final totals and shown as pending; once deduplicated and verified, recompute the historical service date and annotate report revision/cutoff rather than silently adding the call on sync date. Preserve device-time conflict evidence (ADR-002; **Provisional — pending client confirmation**).
- **Returns/cancellations:** order cancellation/return changes net sales, not an already verified call's existence. Voided/invalid visit evidence requires a reason, actor and audit event; recompute dependent KPIs. **Provisional — pending Client question 2.**
