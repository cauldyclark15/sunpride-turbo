## 1. Header and preamble (verbatim)

> To ensure consistency across all territories and improve overall market execution, effective
> February 1, 2026, the following Sales Standardization Guidelines will be implemented. This memo
> serves to standardize sales execution across all territories to improve productivity, coverage
> efficiency, work with standards, and reporting discipline. All sales personnel are required to
> comply with the Sales Operations Standards outlined in this memo and its annexes.

## 2. I. Daily Productive Sales Standards

| Structure / Role                                                                                                                          | Daily Calls | Productive Calls Target |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------- |
| Key Account Specialist (KAS)                                                                                                              | 5           | 90%                     |
| Area Distribution Partner (ADP) / General Trade Booking (GT-Booking)                                                                      | 5           | 90%                     |
| Route Sales (RS) / Area Distribution Partner (ADP) / Public Market Open Trade Extruck (PMOT Extruck) / Public Market Stalls (Pre-booking) | 30          | 85%                     |
| Area Distribution Partner Public Market Stalls (Pre-booking)                                                                              | 30          | 85%                     |

A call is only considered **"Productive"** if the specific execution standards below are met,
based on the channel:

- **Chain Stores (with PO Releasing)** — must have Purchase Order (PO) retrieval, Inventory
  Control (ICO Intervention), Merchandising Fixes, Price Execution, Collection, and Promo Execution.
- **Stores without PO Retrieval** — must have Offtake and Inventory (ICO Fixes), Merchandising
  Fixes, Price Execution, and Promo Validation.
- **PMOT (Public Market / Open Trade)** — must have Sales Generation (Volume/Distribution),
  Merchandising Execution (Display / Price Tagging), Price Compliance, Collection, and Program
  Execution.

## 3. II. Master Coverage Plan (MCP) Assumptions

- **KAS / Booking Personnel** — the MCP must accurately reflect the 5 stores they will be visiting
  for the day and succeeding days as well.
- **Distributor Specialists** — must indicate the Truck they will work with ("Work with" should be
  assessed according to performance and objective as basis).

### Per Diem Validation Policy

- Checking and approval of per diem claims is strictly based on the approved and planned Monthly
  Coverage Plan (MCP).
- Only calls and activities reflected in the MCP, and supported by the required reports and
  documentation, are valid for per diem entitlement.

**Note:** the memo says "Monthly Coverage Plan"; Annex A is titled "Master Coverage Plan (MCP)" and
is laid out as a monthly plan (JANUARY, WEEK 1–5). Same artifact, two names — we should use MCP.

## 4. III. "Work With" Standards and Frequency

Field coaching and supervision with internal SFI and ADP personnel. Minimum frequency:

| Work with Standards               | Weekly | Monthly |
| --------------------------------- | ------ | ------- |
| Sr Channel Development Specialist | 1      | 4       |
| Channel Development Specialist    | 3      | 12      |
| Distributor Specialist            | 4      | 16      |

**Work With Requirements**

- _On training objective work with_
  - a. Must have duly accomplished training logs, both reviewed and discussed with trainee and trainer.
  - b. Observed Basic Call Procedure (BCP) compliance in all Work With, and incorporated with
    Persuasive Selling Format (PSF).
  - c. Trade Development.
- _Sales and Validation Objective_
  - a. Documentation: accomplishment of Distribution, Productivity, Sales Reports (Daily Productive
    Sales Report), or Call Sheets is required. Must be reviewed (pre) before the account call and
    (post) after the account call, with SWOT (Strength, Weakness, Opportunities, Threat) analysis
    remarks or comments.
  - b. Must be an end-to-end work with: if Booking has finished the MCP of the day, the work with is
    done; if truck work with, the MCP must be finished and the person must ride with the truck
    identified for that work with.

## 8. What this settles for Turbo

- **Productive call is now defined by the client, per channel** (Section 2). Our glossary lists
  "Productive call" and "Strike rate" as _pending sign-off_ — this memo is that sign-off, at channel
  granularity, with evidence requirements attached (PO retrieval, ICO interventions, merchandising
  fixes, price execution, collection, promo execution/validation).
- **Target ratios per role** (90% KAS/Booking, 85% field/PMOT) give us the strike-rate target
  dimension: role → daily calls target → productive-call target %.
- **The MCP is a governed, approvable artifact** with a signing loop (Prepared by / Approved by) and
  a direct financial consequence (per diem). It must be versioned per person per month, and planned
  calls must be comparable to actual calls.
- **Roles implied by the memo** (candidate role/position vocabulary): Sales Head; SCDM; Channel
  Development Manager (KAG / Gen Trade); Sr Channel Development Specialist; Channel Development
  Specialist; Distributor Specialist; Key Account Specialist (KAS); Booking; Route Sales (RS);
  Area Distribution Partner (ADP) personnel; PMOT Extruck; Public Market Stalls (pre-booking); RDS
  (ROAR); DSP (coached by DS). Our org-unit model must express "SFI Office" vs "ADP Office" as
  working locations, and "territory" per ADR-005.
- **Deliverable list gives the reporting surface** we owe: DAR, Training Logs, Talk Sheet, Call
  Sheet, ROAR, MCP, DSR, Daily Productive Calls/UBA/OSA/Mandays, Promo Advice vs Allocation,
  Priorities pack, Claims Summary + AR reckoning — with the required medium per report (some are
  "Viber and Hard Copy", i.e. currently outside any system).
- **Work With** is a graded coaching activity with a minimum cadence per supervisory role and its own
  evidence (training logs, BCP/PSF observations, pre/post SWOT review) — a first-class object, not a
  note field on a visit.
- **Annexes A–G are the field form inventory** to digitize; E and G carry status/rating grids that
  map to review-and-close workflows (Annex E's "closes only when Completed" rule is a workflow
  invariant).

## 9. Ambiguities and questions for Sunpride

1. **Section 2, rows 3 and 4 overlap.** Row 3 already contains "Public Market Stalls (Pre-booking)"
   and row 4 repeats it. Is row 3 meant to be RS / ADP / PMOT Extruck only, and row 4 the
   pre-booking stalls? Which roles are actually held to 30 calls/day?
2. **30 calls/day feasibility and definition of a "call".** Does a call count per outlet contact
   (including unsuccessful/no-order contacts), and does the 30 include pre-booked stalls?
3. **ICO** (Intervention / Fixes) — what is the ICO code set and where is it recorded today? We need
   the enumerated values for a productive-call checklist.
4. **Per diem:** rate, currency, claimant roles, and the approval chain. Is a call outside the MCP
   ever payable, and what evidence closes the exception?
5. **MCP naming** — "Monthly Coverage Plan" vs "Master Coverage Plan". Confirm one term.
6. **Work With monthly numbers** = weekly × 4 exactly? And are the minimums per supervisor, per
   supervised person, or per territory?
7. **Role coverage gaps:** the weekly routines cover RS/ADP-DS/CDM, CDM-KAG, KAS+Booking, SCDM only.
   What is the standard week for PMOT Extruck, Public Market Stalls pre-booking, and RDS?
8. **Saturday work and Sunday off** — is Saturday a regular working day for all listed roles, and how
   does that interact with pay/per diem rules (HR/Finance are CC'd)?
9. **Acronyms to be defined and signed off** before we encode them: UBA, OSA, STT, SIV, PC, PDCA/WOR,
   BCP, PSF, ICO, DSP, SFI, RDS, ROAR, DAR, COA, SASR, BR Template, D.A contract, CM.
10. **Annex B/C fidelity:** both are spreadsheet forms exported as low-quality rotated scans. We need
    the original XLSX templates (or a clean re-export) to model their columns exactly — including the
    DSR's per-customer MTD columns and the Call Sheet's week 1–4 inventory columns.
11. **Which annexes are filed daily, weekly, monthly, and by whom** — needed to schedule reminders
    and completeness checks.
12. **Effectiveness:** the memo took effect 2026-02-01. Is this document the currently enforced
    standard, and is there a revision later than 2026-01-20?
