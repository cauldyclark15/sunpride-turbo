To ensure consistency across all territories and improve overall market execution, effective February 1,
2026, the following Sales Standardization Guidelines will be implemented. This memo serves to
standardize sales execution across all territories to improve productivity, coverage efficiency, work with
standards, and reporting discipline. All sales personnel are required to comply with the Sales Operations
Standards outlined in this memo and its annexes.

A call is only considered "Productive" if the specific execution standards below are met based on the
channel:
• Chain Stores (with PO Releasing)
Must have Purchase Order (PO) retrieval, Inventory Control (ICO Intervention), Merchandising
Fixes, Price Execution, Collection, and Promo Execution.
• Stores without PO Retrieval
Must have Offtake and Inventory (ICO Fixes), Merchandising Fixes, Price Execution, and Promo
Validation.
• PMOT (Public Market/Open Trade)
Must have Sales Generation (Volume/Distribution), Merchandising Execution (Display/Price
Tagging), Price Compliance, Collection, and Program Execution.
