# Mobile device loss, transfer and replacement (v1)

**Scope:** registered field devices only. No van POS, connector credential, SAP or financial posting. Follow [`MOBILE_AUTH_V1.md`](../contracts/MOBILE_AUTH_V1.md) before production rollout. Verification evidence: [`docs/qa/DEVICE_REVOCATION_VERIFICATION.md`](../qa/DEVICE_REVOCATION_VERIFICATION.md).

**Console:** Administration → **Phones** lists the phones inside your organizational scope (`mobile/devices:list`), with status, last contact and offline-access expiry. **View phone** opens the lost-device report (`mobile/devices:lostDeviceReport`): what the server received from that phone, the last upload time, and the status history. Key, credential and sign-in subject material never appear there.

1. Treat a lost/stolen/compromised device or private key as urgent. Through an authenticated administrator with `admin.manage` in the device's stored unit (and current unit if transferred):
   - **Suspend** (`devices.suspend({deviceId,reason})`, reasons `lost`, `stolen`, `suspected_compromise`, `other`) when the phone may still turn up. A suspended phone fails every challenge, bind, bootstrap, pull and push. **Reinstate** (`devices.reinstate`, reasons `found`, `other`) only returns it to the same employee in the same unit, and only if no replacement has been enrolled since.
   - **Revoke** (`devices.revoke({deviceId,reason})`, reasons `lost`, `stolen`, `suspected_compromise`, `transfer`, `replacement`, `decommissioned`, `other`) when it is gone for good. Revocation is permanent; a revoked phone can never be reinstated or suspended.
     Reasons are codes, not free text. Record operational details in the restricted incident system, without passwords, tokens, public/private key, GPS or raw proof. If the employee was disabled, a stored-unit admin can still act; an active transfer may require a super admin covering both units. Confirm the status, time and redacted audit entry (`device.suspended`, `device.reinstated`, `device.revoked`) in the phone's history.
2. Check whether the field outbox contains unacknowledged operations. Suspension and revocation immediately deny all **future server requests**; they cannot recover or erase unsent offline work on a lost phone. The lost-device report shows the last contact and the last upload received: **anything recorded on the phone after that time is not on the server.** The phone can keep working offline until its offline-access time (the 24-hour lease), but nothing it records can be uploaded once it is suspended or revoked. Server-side `mobileSyncState`, acknowledged operations and audit history are kept; do not delete them or claim unreceived work was synced. Handle missing visits through an authorized supervisor process. Do not transfer a private key or replay another device's operation IDs to salvage an outbox (the server refuses an operation ID already used by a different device).
3. On the phone, the next online contact after suspension/revocation shows "Phone removed": the app stops sending, holds the unsent outbox and photos for review, and never deletes them silently. If the phone is physically recovered, an administrator may reinstate a suspended phone so the same employee can upload the held work (designed, not yet proven on a real phone); a revoked phone's unsent work must go through supervised recovery under custody.
4. For replacement, revoke the old active device (or leave it suspended) before registering a new one. Enrollment rejects a second active device for the employee or a tag already in use by a non-revoked phone. After outbox reconciliation, generate a new non-exportable P-256 keypair, register the new public key/admin inventory tag, sign and consume a fresh binding challenge online as that employee, then bootstrap again. Old cursor/scope fingerprint must not be reused. Only employee's matching Better Auth bearer plus key possession can bind.
5. On employee transfer, disabled profile, removed effective assignment or unexpected app binding, device authorization fails closed. Investigate assignment history and stored unit/app; do **not** change the old device's `orgUnitId` or subject in place. Revoke and reenroll after management approval. `VAN_ANDROID` cannot enter the field gateway.
6. Recheck with a new bearer and challenge that the suspended/revoked device is denied; do not print bearer, signature or public key in tickets/logs. If a production route accidentally accepts a revoked device, stop mobile writes and escalate with redacted request correlation/time and device ID. Raw location/evidence remains restricted under its own retention rules.

**Operational gaps before release:** no automatic challenge TTL cleanup (challenge issuance and invalid retries are rate limited, see [`MOBILE_RATE_LIMITS_V1.md`](../contracts/MOBILE_RATE_LIMITS_V1.md)); no hardware attestation verifier; no MDM lock/wipe integration (request it from the MDM console by hand); no automatic supervisor/security alert on suspension or revocation; a revoked phone is told `unauthorized` (401) on the sync gateway and confirms "removed" through `devices.mine`. No live DEV end-to-end revocation run on a real phone yet. Restrict enrollment rollout until these and incident ownership are reviewed. A bearer alone is not a device proof, and a device ID alone is not a grant.
