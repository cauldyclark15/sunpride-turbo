# Mobile device loss, transfer and replacement (v1)

**Scope:** registered field devices only. No van POS, connector credential, SAP or financial posting. Routes and DEV bearer smoke are pending slice F; follow [`MOBILE_AUTH_V1.md`](../contracts/MOBILE_AUTH_V1.md) before production rollout.

1. Treat a lost/stolen/compromised device or private key as urgent. Through an authenticated administrator with `admin.manage` in the device's stored unit (and current unit if transferred), call `devices.revoke({deviceId,reason})` with one of `lost`, `stolen`, `suspected_compromise`, `transfer`, `replacement`, `decommissioned`, `other` (a code, not free text). Record operational details in the restricted incident system, without passwords, tokens, public/private key, GPS or raw proof. If the employee was disabled, stored-unit admin can still revoke; an active transfer may require a super admin covering both units. Confirm persisted `status='revoked'`, `statusAt` and redacted audit `device.revoked` for the exact device. A suspended device also cannot authorize, but this slice exposes revocation rather than a public suspend API.
2. Check whether the field outbox contains unacknowledged operations. Revocation immediately denies all **future server requests**, including challenge issuance; it cannot recover or erase unsent offline work on a lost phone. Preserve server-side `mobileSyncState` and audit/history; do not delete them or claim unreceived work was synced. Reconcile accepted operation IDs against the server, and handle missing work through an authorized supervisor process. Do not transfer a private key or replay another device's operation IDs to salvage an outbox.
3. For replacement, revoke the old active device before registering a new one. Enrollment rejects a second active device for the employee or a tag already in use. After outbox reconciliation, generate a new non-exportable P-256 keypair, register the new public key/admin inventory tag, sign and consume a fresh binding challenge online as that employee, then bootstrap again. Old cursor/scope fingerprint must not be reused. Only employee's matching Better Auth bearer plus key possession can bind.
4. On employee transfer, disabled profile, removed effective assignment or unexpected app binding, device authorization fails closed. Investigate assignment history and stored unit/app; do **not** change the old device's `orgUnitId` or subject in place. Revoke and reenroll after management approval. `VAN_ANDROID` cannot enter the field gateway.
5. Recheck with a new bearer and challenge that the revoked device is denied; do not print bearer, signature or public key in tickets/logs. If a production route accidentally accepts a revoked device, stop mobile writes and escalate with redacted request correlation/time and device ID. Raw location/evidence remains restricted under its own retention rules.

**Operational gaps before release:** no automatic challenge TTL cleanup or challenge issuance rate limit; no hardware attestation verifier; no DEV end-to-end native bearer/HTTP identity proof yet. Restrict enrollment rollout until these and incident ownership are reviewed. A bearer alone is not a device proof, and a device ID alone is not a grant.
