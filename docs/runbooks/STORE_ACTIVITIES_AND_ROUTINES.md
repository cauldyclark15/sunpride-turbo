# Store activities and routines

Software workflow for SP-0225, based on the 20 November client review (27:20–28:44, 32:36–33:24 and 35:02–36:48). This is not a record of the client's supplied activity list.

## Office setup

1. Open Setup > Field work > Activities and routines.
2. Add the activities supplied by the office. Collection, Countering and Count stock are meeting examples; they are not automatically installed as approved master data.
3. Add a routine, give it a name, select its activities and save a reason for the change.
4. Edit an activity or routine to rename it or make it inactive. Inactive entries cannot be newly selected. Already saved work keeps its names, including when copied into a plan revision.

The national office maintains this shared list. Other people can read it and choose active entries when planning their own stops.

## Plan a stop

1. Open Coverage plans and the salesperson's month, then the Plan tab.
2. Schedule the customer visits in the dated grid.
3. Expand each visit under Activities at each stop. Select a routine, individual activities, or both. To use only part of a routine, choose Individual activities only and select the activities you need.
4. Press Save dated slots, then submit the plan for the manager's approval.
5. The approved daily route keeps the agreed activity names. Changing the master list does not rewrite an approved stop. Changes to approved work require a plan revision and approval.

## On the Field Android app

1. Sync to download the approved stops. Today lists the activities at today's stops. Tomorrow lists the next day's stops and work as an advance to-do list; it does not start tomorrow's calls.
2. Open today's stop and start the call.
3. Tick only the planned activities actually done. A correction can be unticked while the call is open.
4. The phone saves each change in the same protected queue as the call. Waiting-to-send wording is not confirmation that the office received it. Sync to send it.
5. End the call normally. Its checklist becomes read-only after ending. A refused change says it needs office review rather than claiming success.

## Office follow-up

Open the approved plan's daily routes to compare each agreed activity with Marked done by salesman or Not marked done. These are the salesperson's reports, not independent verification by a manager. An untick replaces the current displayed result but retains the earlier action in the activity history.

A tick for Collection does not record cash, a payment or a receipt. A tick for Count stock does not post inventory. The existing collection, order and stock forms still record those facts; ticking a task alone does not earn productive-call credit or satisfy those forms.

## Verification boundary

Automated checks use a small, clearly labelled local sample: three example activities, one routine, one customer, today's stop and tomorrow's stop. They exercise office setup, plan approval, signed sample-device downloads, tick/untick, repeat delivery and access refusals. This is not a physical-phone acceptance check or proof of public availability. No phone is installed or operated by this work.
