# Forgot your password? Got a temporary password?

**For everyone.** The same email and password work on the website (**sunpride.cauldnclark.com**) and in the Sunpride Field app.

## A. Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page (website or app), tap **Forgot password?**.
2. Type your email. Tap **Send link**.
3. Open the email from **Sunpride Support**. Check your **Spam** folder if you do not see it.
4. Open the link within **60 minutes**.
5. Type your new password twice (at least 8 characters). Tap **Save new password**.
6. Sign in with your new password, on the website and in the app.

*Link expired or already used? You see* **Link no longer valid**. *Tap* **Ask for a new link** *and start again.*

## B. You got a temporary password from the office

7. Sign in with your email and the **temporary password**.
8. The **Set a new password** screen opens by itself.
9. Type the temporary password, then your new password twice.
10. Tap **Set new password**. Use your new password from now on.

*A temporary password works for 3 days. If it expired, ask your office admin or your manager for a new one.*
*In the app, your saved work stays on the phone while you change your password.*

> **If something goes wrong**
> No email after 5 minutes: check Spam, then ask your office admin to give you a temporary password.
> **Too many requests**: wait a few minutes, then try again.
