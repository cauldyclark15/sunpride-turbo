# Update the Sunpride Field app

**For everyone with the phone app.** Updates come from Google Play. Most phones update by themselves.

## A. Let updates install by themselves (do this once)

1. Open the **Play Store**.
2. Tap your profile picture (top right).
3. Tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **Network preferences**, then **Auto-update apps**.
5. Choose **Over any network**. Tap **Done**.

*Updates then install even without Wi-Fi. They may use some mobile data.*

## B. Update right now

6. Open the **Play Store**.
7. Tap your profile picture (top right).
8. Tap **Manage apps & device**.
9. Tap **Update all**. Or find **Sunpride Field** and tap **Update**.
10. Open **Sunpride Field**. Sign in as usual if it asks.

## C. Check which version you have

11. In the app, tap **More** at the bottom.
12. The version is at the very bottom, under **Sign out** (for example *Sunpride Field 1.0*). **Support info** shows it too.

**Do NOT uninstall the app.** Uninstalling deletes customers and other work that are not sent yet. An update keeps everything.

> **If something goes wrong**
> No **Update** button and the office says there is a new version: wait a few minutes, close the Play Store, open it again.
> Google Play does not work: use the backup folder https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/181uAAjyMxw08WoDKSGaumrTPPZvQA282 . Download the newest file, open it, tap **Update**. Do not uninstall first.
